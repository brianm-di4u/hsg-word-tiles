## Ten remaining fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Board tile glyphs and point values — entire placed/staged grid.** Darken letters toward the rack's ink, strengthen small numerals, and reduce lighting that washes out ink. Preserve a clear pending-state outline without greying the word. Validate both unzoomed phone and desktop (desktop/v03-staging.png; mobile/v04-midgame.png). This affects every move.
2. **Premium legend — help's five example icons.** Render the actual board symbols: cream letter discs, dark word discs, matching rings and numerals, and an actual bathhouse plaque for Row. Remove the unrelated gradient/square substitutes (mobile/v07-help-squares.png; mobile/v02-board-start.png). The explanation must teach what players see.
3. **Phone header and Featured Springs — top of gameplay.** Combine turn status and scores more tightly and reduce the plaques' vertical chrome; return height to the board area while retaining readable clue letters. The current separate rows delay the playing surface excessively (mobile/v02-board-start.png; mobile/v04-midgame.png).
4. **Zoom/loupe composition — phone board viewport.** Use the same marble color and rendering as the underlying square; make the relationship between the loupe's target and floating dragged tile explicit with a restrained guide or matched target marker. Simplify the overview into clear occupied cells and a strong viewport boundary (mobile/v10-loupe.png). The capture's red X can be valid rejection feedback; its presence alone is not a defect.
5. **Ledger viewport — lower edge of desktop right panel.** Reserve space for complete rows or provide a deliberate scroll treatment and bottom padding. Do not expose half a line as the terminal state. Keep the score and move on the same visible row (desktop/v04-midgame.png; desktop/v09-night.png).
6. **Board frame, countertop and props — desktop left region.** Replace broad uniform brown bands with subtler material variation and more coherent edge/contact shadows. Give the towel visible softness and let the bell share the board's illumination. Avoid adding more decorative trim (desktop/v02-board-start.png; desktop/v09-night.png).
7. **Bathhouse plaques — eight named board squares.** Simplify each silhouette and increase the usable name strip relative to the artwork. At phone overview size, use a clear Row-family marker with detail available in zoom rather than pretending microscopic names are readable (mobile/v02-board-start.png; desktop/v04-midgame.png).
8. **Register discovery hierarchy — category grids.** Reduce the visual weight of repeated locked cards, distinguish discovered entries more strongly, and keep category progress near the entries. On phone, the large heading and a full screen of nearly identical locks bury the collection's interesting content (mobile/v08-register.png; desktop/v08-register.png).
9. **Focus and button contours — fact Close and primary actions.** Keep a visible keyboard focus outline, but simplify nested gold strokes and stacked bevel lines into one consistent border plus a separated focus ring. Match the treatment across Close, Start and Play again (mobile/v05-fact.png; desktop/v11-setup.png; mobile/v06-over.png).
10. **Results composition — lower half of phone winner and summary.** Tighten the large gap before Hot Springs words and the sparse lower summary so the replay actions feel attached to the result. Retain the successful portrait/name/score reflow (mobile/v06-over.png). On desktop, make the summary sections share a clearer baseline and spacing rhythm (desktop/v06-over.png).

## Outright visible defects

These are separate from subjective opportunities to improve materials or composition.

- **Help icon/rule mismatch:** help says cream versus dark discs, but its examples do not depict those discs; the live board does (desktop/v07-help-squares.png; mobile/v07-help-squares.png; mobile/v02-board-start.png).
- **Ledger row clipping:** a partial next entry is cut at the rounded lower boundary (desktop/v04-midgame.png; desktop/v09-night.png). This is a presentation defect, not evidence that entries are inaccessible.
- **Loupe material discontinuity:** grey/white marble inside the lens differs from the warm cream board in the same state (mobile/v10-loupe.png).
- **Weak small board text:** placed/staged ink and corner values are markedly fainter than rack text; tiny bathhouse captions collapse at normal phone scale (desktop/v03-staging.png; mobile/v04-midgame.png; mobile/v02-board-start.png). This is a visible legibility issue, not a measured accessibility contrast failure.

No claim is made about invisible hitboxes, animation, scrolling, invalid-word logic, or historical accuracy of the illustration. The long help and Register captures do not establish unreachable content. The output is a visual review, not a runtime test.
