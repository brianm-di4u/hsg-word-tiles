## Ten remaining fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Phone board cells and small labels — central play surface.** Keep the overview, but give zoom an explicit, legible entry affordance in the overview and increase point/premium label clarity in zoom. Reduce incidental texture behind tiny values. The overview in `mobile/v04-midgame.png` asks too much of roughly 23 px cells; the only captured zoom control is already in the zoomed state in `mobile/v10-loupe.png`. Discoverability outside that capture is unverified.
2. **Featured Springs — phone area between scores and board.** Replace the large two-tier gold plaque composition with a quieter compact arrangement, retaining readable revealed letters; shorten or relocate the explanatory sentence. In `mobile/v02-board-start.png` and `mobile/v04-midgame.png`, this secondary puzzle occupies a large, high-contrast region before the actual board. Recover room for history and play feedback.
3. **Disabled Play — bottom-right action in both themes.** Use one unmistakably unavailable treatment with the same visual meaning across themes. Remove gold emphasis and the strong black check from night disabled Play (`mobile/v09-night.png`, `desktop/v09-night.png`); maintain readable text while reducing visual priority.
4. **Bathhouse Row symbol — board, help and fact-card category.** Adopt one recognizable silhouette everywhere. The domed building in `desktop/v02-board-start.png` and arch in `desktop/v07-help-squares.png` / `mobile/v05-fact.png` should match, including at board-cell size. This repairs the legend-to-board mapping before adding more ornament.
5. **Board frame and marble — desktop play field.** Reduce the number and width of flat brown/gold bevel bands; introduce a restrained shared light direction, edge reflection and contact shading. Preserve letter contrast. `desktop/v02-board-start.png` needs stone and timber to feel as materially integrated as `barons-desktop-board3d.png`; simply deepening veins would add noise.
6. **Loupe feedback cluster — upper-left of zoomed phone board.** Give target, dragged tile and invalid status distinct positions and jobs; simplify the large ring and red outline combination, and anchor TAKEN consistently beside the target. In `mobile/v10-loupe.png`, the floating A covers underlying letters and the magnified C plus badge compete with the target context. Preserve the useful minimap.
7. **START disc — board center.** Replace white decorative lettering on light turquoise with a darker, simpler label and sufficient breathing room (`mobile/v02-board-start.png`, `desktop/v02-board-start.png`). It fits now, but fitting alone does not make it clearly readable.
8. **Register locked entries — main collection grid.** Reduce dashed-outline repetition and add a clear, intentional locked-entry treatment plus stronger category navigation/progress hierarchy. In `mobile/v08-register.png`, almost the whole screen becomes a wall of pale placeholders; `desktop/v08-register.png` shows the same issue across four columns. Keep hidden words hidden while making the collection feel designed.
9. **Night counter and props — behind the desktop board.** Retain subdued wood grain and tune towel/brass highlights to the same light source as the board. `desktop/v09-night.png` loses the counter’s tactile presence while isolated props remain conspicuous. Use `barons-desktop-dark.png` as the visible reference for retaining material context in darkness.
10. **Phone Steam ledger — bottom panel.** Rebalance the recovered vertical space to show more than two entries and retain concise bonus context beside each applicable entry. `mobile/v04-midgame.png` omits the visible desktop Hot Springs +10/+20 sublines and shows only two plays; `desktop/v04-midgame.png` demonstrates the useful information being lost. Do not shrink the already small type to accomplish this.

## Outright visible defects

- **Legend/board icon mismatch:** Bathhouse Row is an arch in `desktop/v07-help-squares.png` and `mobile/v07-help-squares.png`, a domed building in `desktop/v02-board-start.png` and `mobile/v02-board-start.png`. This contradicts the change-log claim that help uses the same mark.
- **Disabled-state visual inconsistency:** unavailable Play is muted brown in `mobile/v04-midgame.png` and `desktop/v04-midgame.png`, gold with black content in `mobile/v09-night.png` and `desktop/v09-night.png`. This contradicts the claimed single disabled style and makes state interpretation less reliable.
- **Weak START label contrast:** pale lettering lies over a pale mottled turquoise center disc in `mobile/v02-board-start.png` and `desktop/v02-board-start.png`. It is contained, but visually weak precisely where the opening instruction sends the eye.

No demonstrated major overlap or cropped board frame remains. Help and Register extend below the screenshot viewport (`desktop/v07-help-squares.png`, `mobile/v08-register.png`); this is observable, but is not proof that scrolling is broken or content inaccessible. The floating A occluding letters in the loupe may be intentional drag feedback; its exact interaction behavior is a hypothesis, not a confirmed functional defect. No functional defects or animation failures are inferred from still images.
