## Ten remaining fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Board tile faces and point values — desktop/mobile board interior.** Give occupied tiles stronger dark edges, a distinguishable face tone, darker letters, and larger bold values; reduce marble contrast behind them. Preserve the excellent rack readability. Check ordinary overview scale, not just zoom. Evidence: `desktop/v04-midgame.png`, `mobile/v04-midgame.png`.
2. **Loupe warning — below the lens in phone zoom.** Move the occupied-target label above or beside the lens, reserve space outside the dragged tile, and keep the entire warning visible. The original capture's A covers the rust pill. Evidence: `mobile/v10-loupe.png`.
3. **Featured Springs plaques — phone upper band.** Compress the three mostly empty full-width gold rows into a quieter compact group, retaining readable revealed letters; use recovered space to improve board scale or provide an explicit zoom entry. Evidence: `mobile/v02-board-start.png`, `mobile/v04-midgame.png`.
4. **Premium-square symbols — normal board scale.** Simplify pane detail, strengthen the cream/dark disc distinction, and enlarge multiplier numerals within the square. The help examples are legible; the same art shrinks to visual confetti on the phone board. Evidence: `mobile/v07-help-squares.png`, `mobile/v02-board-start.png`.
5. **Help dialog navigation — top-right and lower viewport edge.** Add a persistent visible Close control and an unmistakable scroll cue for the long rules body. Avoid a panel that appears to run offscreen without visible navigation. Evidence: `desktop/v07-help-squares.png`, `mobile/v07-help-squares.png`.
6. **Board frame and table — desktop left region.** Replace broad brown gradients and repeated bevel bands with subtler material texture, narrower frame depth, and more consistent contact lighting for board, bag, and bell. Evidence: `desktop/v02-board-start.png`, `desktop/v09-night.png`; compare `benchmarks/barons-desktop-board3d.png`.
7. **Last-move outline — WEEKLONG across mid-board.** Use a stronger continuous outline or a distinct edge treatment that survives the cream/gold palette and remains distinct from teal staging. Evidence: `desktop/v04-midgame.png`, `mobile/v09-night.png`.
8. **Register locked entries — central collection grid.** Collapse or summarize locked rows, add category navigation and a compact progress overview, and bring found entries from later categories into view sooner. Preserve letter-count clues where useful, but stop giving every unknown word a full card's visual weight. Evidence: `desktop/v08-register.png`, `mobile/v08-register.png`.
9. **Bonus strip — between board and rack/sidebar actions.** Replace the compressed “TUB +20 · Row ×2” presentation with an explicit point breakdown that states what doubled and how it relates to the displayed bonus. Give the card link a clearer secondary role. Evidence: `desktop/v04-midgame.png`, `mobile/v04-midgame.png`. This is a clarity fix; the images do not establish a scoring calculation bug.
10. **In-play place identity — board frame and bathhouse medallions.** Add a restrained Bathhouse Letters/Hot Springs maker's plaque to the frame and make a selected medallion's bathhouse identity visibly available on phones. Tiny architectural circles alone cannot carry the specific local identity achieved by the title and fact card. Evidence: `desktop/v02-board-start.png`, `mobile/v02-board-start.png`.

## Outright visible defects

- **Occluded occupied-target warning:** `mobile/v10-loupe.png`, beneath the lens. The floating A covers the rust warning label, so its message cannot be read. This is the clearest actual overlap defect.
- **Board annotation readability failure:** `mobile/v04-midgame.png` and `mobile/v09-night.png`, small point values at tile corners. At the intended phone presentation they are too small and faint for reliable reading; rack values are visibly much stronger. Desktop board values are also weak in `desktop/v04-midgame.png`.

The missing visible Help close/overflow affordance is documented above, but an image cannot establish whether dismissal or scrolling is unavailable. Register continuation below the viewport is not by itself a clipping bug. No interaction failures, source-history errors, or animation defects are inferred from these PNGs.
