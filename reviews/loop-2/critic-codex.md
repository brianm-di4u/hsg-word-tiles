# Bathhouse Letters — adversarial visual review, loop 2

**49/80. Gates fail: six of eight categories are below 7.** Independently judged from the current captures, using RUBRIC.md and the loop-1 change log as context, not as credit for effort. The previous 43/80 is not a scoring floor. This set is substantially more coherent than a prototype, but the playing surface and phone information hierarchy do not yet match the finish of the benchmark.

## Scope and calibration

Inspected all 21 supplied captures individually: desktop and mobile `v01-title`, `v11-setup`, `v07-help-squares`, `v08-register`, `v02-board-start`, `v03-staging`, `v04-midgame`, `v05-fact`, `v06-over`, `v09-night`, plus mobile `v10-loupe`. Capture references below are relative to `screenshots/visual-2/` and omit `.png`.

Compared all seven benchmark PNGs: `barons-desktop-board3d`, `barons-desktop-dark`, `barons-desktop-fact`, `barons-desktop-over`, `barons-desktop-title`, `barons-mobile-board`, and `barons-mobile-title`. A 7 means comparable finish in the category, not identical styling. Barons is imperfect too: its tiny perimeter captions and dense phone board are not models to copy. Its advantage is the continuous authored illustration, stronger material separation, and clear hierarchy between game actions and decoration. Letters' title and magnolia card reach that quality; its repeated marble grid and anonymous gold hint strips do not.

This is a static visual assessment. Touch behavior, scrolling, animation, sound, and transient timing were not tested. Phone PNGs are 780 pixels wide for the stated 390-pixel viewport; apparent target sizes must be divided by two. Content continuing below a screenshot is not by itself proof of inaccessible content.

## Ten highest-impact remaining fixes, ranked

1. **Give the move announcement and fact banner exclusive ownership of the feedback region.** Region: immediately below the board on phones. In `mobile/v09-night`, two complete messages occupy the same pixels. Replace/queue the previous message atomically, or allocate separate rows with sufficient height; retain readable foreground/background pairs throughout any transition. Verify this exact night state and the equivalent day state.

2. **Redesign premium-square labels for actual board scale.** Region: the 15×15 playfield, especially `mobile/v02-board-start` and `desktop/v02-board-start`. The larger disc still contains LETTER/WORD microtext. Use a compact, bold, distinct letter/word code with the multiplier; move bathhouse names into an on-demand enlarged label. Keep ornate spring rays secondary to the scoring symbol. Aim for immediate differentiation without opening Help or zooming.

3. **Make placed tiles read as pieces above marble.** Region: occupied board cells in `desktop/v04-midgame`, `mobile/v04-midgame`, and `mobile/v09-night`. Darken letter strokes, strengthen face-to-marble value separation, and give a restrained consistent bevel/contact shadow. Reduce the gray haze that makes installed letters look weaker than rack letters. Preserve a clear staging outline without making committed tiles appear disabled.

4. **Reflow the phone winner card.** Region: gold panel in `mobile/v06-over`. Put portrait and winner label/name on a generous top row; put score and best-word/result details below, instead of reserving three rigid columns. “Maestro Julian Fitch” should not become a tall three-line column squeezed against 365. Keep desktop's compact horizontal arrangement in `desktop/v06-over`.

5. **Rebuild the ledger bottom treatment.** Region: lower edge of Steam ledger in `desktop/v02-board-start`, `desktop/v04-midgame`, and their phone equivalents. Remove the dark inset-looking smear. If overflow needs a fade, blend into the panel's cream or night surface and confine it to overflow states; use padding so a partly hidden row does not masquerade as the last readable entry. Separate actor, words, and points more clearly when long opponent names fill the row.

6. **Restore meaning to phone Featured Springs.** Region: three gold plaques above the board in `mobile/v02-board-start` and `mobile/v04-midgame`. Restore a compact shared heading with +30 and per-category symbols, or add short labels. Reduce plaque padding/shadow so the anonymous gold bars do not compete with the board and scores. Desktop already provides the missing context; adapt it deliberately.

7. **Unify board and counter materials.** Region: frame, marble, and surrounding counter in `desktop/v02-board-start` and `desktop/v09-night`; enlarged veins in `mobile/v10-loupe`. Vary the marble sampling so adjacent squares do not advertise repetition. Simplify the stacked frame bands, add softer shared illumination, and ground bell/towel with matching shadows. Night should preserve depth through lighting rather than making the marble cold gray while the frame stays brass-brown.

8. **Make the Register look intentionally locked, not unfinished.** Region: dashed pip entries in `desktop/v08-register` and `mobile/v08-register`. Add a concise locked-state key and give entries a restrained ledger treatment—rules, numbering, or a faint seal—in place of dozens of empty dashed containers. Keep found words prominent, but avoid making rows grow irregularly simply because one cell contains a discovery date. The current majority state resembles a loading skeleton.

9. **Consolidate control emphasis across themes.** Region: Play, reward banner, secondary actions, and dialog Close in `desktop/v09-night`, `desktop/v03-staging`, and `mobile/v05-fact`. Define one primary-action treatment and a quieter reward treatment for each palette. Simplify focus to a clearly separated ring without the stacked yellow border halo; keep it visible. Gold should not simultaneously signal frame ornament, reward, focus, and primary action at equal strength.

10. **Give the loupe spatial context.** Region: circular magnifier in `mobile/v10-loupe`. Retain the red taken-square cross, but show neighboring cells or an unobtrusive row/column reference with the target marker. Ensure the dragged tile and inspection target have an obvious visual relationship. The current view makes the invalid cell clear while leaving its position within the larger board hard to recover. This is a visual improvement request, not a claim that dragging is broken.

## Outright defects observed

- **Overlapping feedback text:** `mobile/v09-night`, below the board. The AI move announcement and TUB fact banner occupy the same region and make each other harder to read. The capture proves the overlap; its duration and underlying cause cannot be determined here.
- **Ledger content rendered into the bottom fade:** `desktop/v04-midgame` and `desktop/v09-night`, lowest visible history row; `mobile/v04-midgame`, bottom ledger text/rules. The dark gradient obscures content rather than terminating it cleanly. The corresponding empty ledger in `desktop/v02-board-start` and `mobile/v02-board-start` also carries the dark band.

The small premium captions, repeated marble, crowded winner name, and anonymous phone plaques are design shortcomings included in the ranked fixes, not additional claims of broken behavior. Help/Register continue below the viewport; these captures do not establish a scrolling or dismissal defect. No animation, touch-target response, contrast-ratio compliance, or game-rule defect is asserted from screenshots alone.
