## Ten fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Rebuild the mobile fact toast as a compact, non-overlapping notification.** In the top-center region of `mobile/v04-midgame.png` and `mobile/v09-night.png`, the current narrow card grows down across the header, scores, clues, and board. Reserve a full-width notification row outside those regions, use a short bonus summary, and move the historical sentence into the linked fact card. Preserve readable scores and the complete play area while it is visible.
2. **Simplify the default phone board's micro-labels and recover board space.** In `mobile/v02-board-start.png` and `mobile/v03-staging.png`, reduce frame thickness and replace stacked premium captions with large, distinct multiplier symbols; expose full square names in a detail/zoom treatment. Keep warm/hot letter bonuses distinguishable from word bonuses without relying only on color. This addresses information that is currently illegible at the normal board scale.
3. **Replace the fact card's generic icon slab with subject-specific art.** In the left third of `desktop/v05-fact.png` and upper block of `mobile/v05-fact.png`, show a magnolia/promenade illustration or image for MAGNOLIA, with a much smaller category badge. Tighten the mobile art block so the actual fact remains the focal point. Match the contextual richness of `barons-desktop-fact.png`.
4. **Fix the selected opponent field on phones.** In `mobile/v11-setup.png`, separate the character name, role, and difficulty into readable lines or a compact selected-character card instead of cutting the option off inside the select. In `desktop/v11-setup.png`, make the two Person/Computer segments fill their container evenly rather than leaving a long unlabelled tail. Both changes should make the current selection visually unambiguous.
5. **Give results a distinctive bathhouse editorial composition.** In `desktop/v06-over.png` and `mobile/v06-over.png`, add a register/guestbook masthead, a larger winner medallion, and a deliberate score/rank hierarchy. Group revealed featured words into a compact summary instead of a loose set of beige chips. Bring the finish closer to `barons-desktop-over.png` while retaining Letters' own identity.
6. **Finish the counter, frame, and peripheral prop crop.** In `desktop/v02-board-start.png`, remove the abrupt rectangular wood-tone/grain transitions, brighten selected frame bevels, and improve the contact shadow so the board reads as one crafted object. In `mobile/v02-board-start.png` and `mobile/v10-loupe.png`, hide or intentionally recompose the towel; remove the isolated white sliver at the left boundary.
7. **Make ledger overflow look intentional and fully readable.** At the lower right of `desktop/v04-midgame.png` and bottom of `mobile/v04-midgame.png`, reserve internal bottom padding and use a clear scroll boundary/affordance so a row does not collide with the rounded mask. Preserve a full visible final row where possible. The captures do not establish whether scrolling already exists; the requested fix concerns the visibly unfinished edge treatment.
8. **Make the Register feel collected rather than mostly empty.** In `desktop/v08-register.png` and `mobile/v08-register.png`, shrink locked-word entries, group them more compactly, and feature signed entries with a small stamp or subject vignette. Keep word-length hints but reduce the wall of large dashed placeholders; on mobile the first category occupies essentially the whole capture before Bathhouse Row can appear.
9. **Unify controls and focus across day/night.** In `desktop/v05-fact.png` and `mobile/v05-fact.png`, replace the layered cyan/gold Close outline with a clear theme-matched focus treatment that remains conspicuous. In `desktop/v09-night.png`, reduce the brightness of secondary gold clue plaques and harmonize their edges with the dark panels; retain the strongest gold emphasis for the primary action. Normalize border thickness and raised-edge treatment across tabs, selectors, and buttons.
10. **Reflow help rows and clarify the loupe's spatial context.** In `mobile/v07-help-squares.png`, use an icon/name heading with the explanation beneath it rather than splitting each sentence into narrow columns; eliminate the stranded leading colon in the Bathhouse Row explanation. In `mobile/v10-loupe.png`, add a small board-location indicator and a clear target-cell outline distinct from the floating dragged tile, so the enlarged fragment is easier to orient within the full board. The current still does not establish drag behavior or snapping accuracy.

## Outright defects and visible readability failures

- **Overlap, severe:** `mobile/v04-midgame.png`, `mobile/v09-night.png` — reward toast masks turn/bag text, score chips, featured clues, and upper board cells. This is visible in both themes, independent of its unknown duration.
- **Clipped selection:** `mobile/v11-setup.png` — opponent text ends abruptly after the role separator/initial of the difficulty; the selected difficulty is not readable.
- **Clipped ledger rows:** `desktop/v04-midgame.png`, `desktop/v09-night.png` — bottom ACME/TA entry runs into the ledger's lower rounded boundary. `mobile/v04-midgame.png`, `mobile/v09-night.png` — bottom GRUB/BRUSHER entry is visibly cut at the panel boundary. Scrolling availability is unknown; permanent data loss is not claimed.
- **Stray cropped prop:** `mobile/v02-board-start.png`, `mobile/v03-staging.png`, `mobile/v10-loupe.png` — small white towel fragment at the left edge of the board region reads as an accidental remnant of the desktop composition.
- **Unreadable miniature captions at normal phone scale:** `mobile/v02-board-start.png`, `mobile/v04-midgame.png` — bathhouse names, premium descriptive text, and center-medallion wording are too small to read comfortably. Letter faces are more readable; the claim is specifically about the subsidiary captions. `mobile/v10-loupe.png` confirms that enlarging the cells reveals the detail.
- **Awkward alignment/reflow:** `mobile/v07-help-squares.png` — Bathhouse Row's explanation starts with a detached colon in the right column, and Boiling Spring wraps into two lines beside a separately wrapped explanation. This is a visibly broken reading rhythm, not a missing-content claim.
- **Weak visual separation in night mode:** `desktop/v09-night.png`, `mobile/v09-night.png` — muted gray tile faces and blue-gray marble reduce tile/background separation compared with day mode. No numerical contrast-ratio or accessibility-conformance claim is made from the PNGs.

The help and Register captures continue below their bottom edges (`desktop/v07-help-squares.png`, `mobile/v07-help-squares.png`, `desktop/v08-register.png`, `mobile/v08-register.png`). That alone is not proof of inaccessible content or broken scrolling, so it is not counted as an outright defect. The cropped board perimeter in the loupe is also expected for a zoomed view. The title art is a relative strength; copying the benchmark's title composition would have less impact than repairing the mobile obstruction and upgrading secondary screens.
